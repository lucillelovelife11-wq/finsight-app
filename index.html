ROE & Three-Factor DuPont Analysis

ROE = Return on Sales × Total Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage. Use average balance-sheet denominators when available.

Financial inputs

Calculated drivers

ROE = (NI / Sales) × (Sales / Avg. Assets) × (Avg. Assets / Avg. Equity)

NI and equity should be attributable to the same shareholder group. Negative or zero average equity makes standard ROE interpretation problematic.

Operating ROA & Financial Leverage

Separates operating performance from financing and non-operating investments.

Operating inputs

Operating performance

NOPAT = NI + (Interest expense − Interest revenue) × (1 − tax rate)
NOA = Total assets − Operating liabilities − Non-operating assets
OpROA = NOPAT / NOA

This calculator uses a simplified after-tax net interest adjustment. The course also emphasizes classification judgments and the need to match flow with invested operating capital. For a period analysis, replace the single balance with average NOA where appropriate.

Accruals & Cash Flow Quality

The course defines accruals as Net Income − Operating Cash Flow. Differences are diagnostic, not proof of manipulation.

Income-to-cash bridge

Diagnostics

Accruals = NI − OCF
FCF (simple) = OCF − CapEx

The simplified receivables change is not a substitute for credit-sales turnover or allowance analysis. Compare across periods and consider business model, non-cash compensation, working capital, and one-time items.

Discounted Cash Flow · Perpetuity Growth

Illustrative enterprise-value DCF using projected free cash flow to the firm (FCFF), discount rate and terminal growth.

Assumptions

DCF outputs

Terminal Value₅ = FCFF₆ / (WACC − g)
Enterprise Value = PV(explicit FCFF) + PV(terminal value)

Assumes constant annual growth for five years and a stable perpetual terminal rate. Equity value = enterprise value − net debt; other claims and non-operating assets may require adjustments in real valuations.
